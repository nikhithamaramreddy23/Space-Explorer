👨‍🚀 Neil Armstrong

Neil Armstrong was the first human to walk on the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission in 1969.

👨‍🚀 Buzz Aldrin

Buzz Aldrin was the second human to walk on the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission.

👨‍🚀 Yuri Gagarin

Yuri Gagarin was the first human to travel into outer space in 1961 aboard Vostok 1.

👩‍🚀 Kalpana Chawla

Kalpana Chawla was an Indian-American astronaut who flew aboard the Space Shuttle Columbia.

👨‍🚀 Rakesh Sharma

Rakesh Sharma was the first Indian citizen to travel into space in 1984.
