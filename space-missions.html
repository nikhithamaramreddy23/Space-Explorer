Mission Year Organization Purpose

🌕 Apollo 11

Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land humans on the Moon. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface in 1969.

🚀 Voyager 1

Voyager 1 was launched in 1977 to study the outer planets and eventually travel into interstellar space.

🔴 Perseverance

NASA's Perseverance rover landed on Mars in 2021. It studies the Martian surface and searches for signs of ancient life.
